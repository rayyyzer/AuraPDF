Design the complete UI/UX for AuraPDF, a premium AI-powered PDF workspace.

Do NOT make this look like a generic PDF reader, document management dashboard, or an obvious clone of Adobe Acrobat. Create a distinctive, modern product identity that feels like a serious next-generation productivity application.

Your job is to make the major design decisions autonomously. Do not wait for me to specify every component, layout, color, spacing value, or interaction. Use strong product-design judgment and create a cohesive system.

## PRODUCT VISION

AuraPDF lets users work with PDFs in one intelligent workspace.

Core capabilities should include:

- Uploading and importing PDFs
- Browsing and organizing documents
- Reading PDFs
- Searching within documents
- AI-powered document understanding
- Asking questions about a document
- Summarizing documents
- Extracting important information
- Highlighting and annotating
- Editing PDFs where appropriate
- Managing multiple documents
- Recent documents
- Favorites/pinned documents
- Document organization
- Settings and account management

The experience should communicate:

Intelligent. Premium. Calm. Fast. Focused.

The AI should feel naturally embedded into the PDF workflow rather than bolted on as a generic chatbot.

# 2. VISUAL IDENTITY

Create a distinctive visual identity for AuraPDF.

Avoid excessive gradients, excessive glassmorphism, overly rounded SaaS cards, or visual clutter.

Prioritize:

- Excellent typography
- Strong hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Subtle depth
- Refined surfaces
- Precise alignment
- Sophisticated contrast
- Restrained use of accent colors
- Elegant micro-interactions

The interface should look excellent both in light and dark themes.

Make the design feel expensive and intentional without becoming flashy.

# 7. AI ASSISTANT

Create AuraPDF's defining AI experience.

Do NOT design it as a generic ChatGPT clone.

The AI should understand the current document and feel contextual to it.

Create an integrated AI panel containing:

- Conversation
- Suggested questions
- Document-aware responses
- Citations/references to pages
- "Jump to source" interactions
- Summarize
- Explain
- Extract
- Compare
- Rewrite
- Key points

Example contextual suggestions:

"Summarize this document"

"What are the key findings?"

"Explain this section"

"Find all mentions of X"

"Create a table of the important data"

"Compare these two documents"

Make citations visually connected to the actual PDF pages.

The AI panel should be able to coexist beside the PDF without destroying the reading experience.
